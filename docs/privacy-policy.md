# BAJ E-POD Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026.

## About this policy

BAJ E-POD is an electronic proof-of-delivery and waybill management application published by Essuman Technologies for BAJ Freight & Logistics Limited. This policy explains how personal information is handled when staff, drivers, delivery recipients, and other authorized participants use the app or provide delivery information.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **essumantechnologies@gmail.com** and include “BAJ E-POD privacy request” in the subject.

## Information handled by the app

- **Account information:** names, email addresses, account identifiers, assigned roles, departments, account status, and account creation or update dates. Authorized administrators create and manage staff accounts. Login credentials are processed for authentication; temporary account credentials may also be managed by administrators during account setup.
- **Delivery and waybill information:** driver names and identifiers, vehicle registration numbers, consignee and recipient names, delivery addresses, recipient email addresses and phone numbers, shipment references, cargo details, delivery remarks, delivery status, and relevant dates.
- **Proof of delivery:** handwritten driver and recipient signatures, images of recipient stamps, and documents generated from delivery records. These images are used as evidence of delivery; the app does not use them for biometric identity recognition.
- **Activity records:** information about who created, updated, assigned, delivered, invoiced, or marked a record as deleted, together with associated timestamps.
- **Device and service information:** service providers may process network information such as IP addresses and technical information needed to authenticate requests, deliver content, and operate their services. Locally saved records and settings support offline use and synchronization.

Delivery addresses are entered as part of delivery records. The current Android app does not request device GPS-location permission or access to the device address book.

## How information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, apply account roles, create and assign waybills, record and verify deliveries, capture proof of delivery, synchronize records, generate PDFs and reports, support invoicing workflows, communicate delivery documents, and investigate support or operational issues.

## Who can receive information

Authorized staff and administrators can access records according to the app’s configured permissions and operational responsibilities. Delivery information may also be provided to relevant customers, recipients, drivers, and business participants as part of the delivery workflow.

The app uses these services:

- **Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore** for authentication, account profiles, cloud records, and synchronization. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
- **Cloudinary** for hosting uploaded signature and stamp images. The application stores image links in delivery records. People who receive these links may be able to view the associated images. See [Cloudinary’s privacy policy](https://cloudinary.com/privacy).
- **Configured email providers** for sending signed waybill PDFs to recipients and configured copy recipients in earlier app versions. Email sending is disabled in version 1.0.2.
- **WhatsApp**, when a user initiates document sharing through an installed WhatsApp application. Shared documents and recipient details are then handled by that service. See [WhatsApp’s privacy policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy).

The reviewed app does not contain an advertising SDK. Sharing delivery records with business participants and service providers is part of operating the service.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to respond to a lawful request or protect rights and security.

## Camera, images, and local storage

The app requests camera access when needed to capture images for a delivery record. Users may select an image through the device’s image picker. Selected or captured stamp images and signatures may be uploaded and included in delivery documents.

Delivery records, pending updates, images, and settings may be stored on the device to support offline operation and synchronization. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app can remove local information, including unsynchronized records; it does not automatically delete cloud records or copies already shared with others.

## Retention and deletion

Delivery records and associated proof-of-delivery information are retained for 3 years for operational, delivery-evidence, accounting, and dispute-resolution purposes, unless a longer period is required by applicable law or an unresolved legal matter. Account information is retained as needed to administer authorized access and associated business records.

Marking a waybill as deleted in the app can remove it from normal views while retaining it in cloud records. Disabling an account does not necessarily erase the account or its associated delivery history.

To request access, correction, account deletion, or deletion of personal information, contact **essumantechnologies@gmail.com** or your organization’s administrator. Identify the relevant account or delivery record without sending your password. Requests are handled through essumantechnologies@gmail.com and require identity and authorization checks. Where records must be retained, the response should explain the reason and applicable retention period. Copies previously sent to recipients or external services may require separate requests to those recipients or services.

## Security and international processing

The app uses authenticated accounts and configured access permissions. Firebase and Cloudinary connections use HTTPS. Email sending is disabled in version 1.0.2. In earlier versions, email transport protection depends on the organization’s configured email service and settings. No system can guarantee absolute security; users should protect their credentials and devices and report suspected unauthorized access to the support address.

Service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country. Their processing locations and practices depend on their service arrangements and configurations.

## Children

BAJ E-POD is intended for authorized business users and delivery operations, rather than children. If you believe a child’s personal information was provided inappropriately, contact the support address so the matter can be reviewed.
